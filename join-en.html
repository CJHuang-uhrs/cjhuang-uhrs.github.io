Research collaboration & recruitment

The Chengjun Huang group welcomes applications for postdoctoral researcher and research assistant positions in neurorehabilitation engineering. Applicants with backgrounds in rehabilitation engineering, biomedical engineering, electronic information engineering, or related disciplines are encouraged to contact us. Experience in surface EMG or sleep EEG analysis is preferred. Postdoctoral applicants must hold a doctoral degree; research assistant applicants must hold a master’s degree.

Please send your CV, representative publications, and relevant materials to huangchengjun@uhrs.edu.cn, with the email subject “University + Name + Postdoctoral/Research Assistant Application”.
