Research Interests

My research combines bioelectric signal analysis, computational modeling, and experimental studies to assess function and investigate mechanisms in neurorehabilitation.

  • Characterization of motor unit properties and localization of muscle innervation zones using high-density surface EMG.
  • Analysis of sleep brain state dynamics and detection of biomarkers using EEG.
  • Assessment of limb motor control and functional impairment using EEG and EMG.
