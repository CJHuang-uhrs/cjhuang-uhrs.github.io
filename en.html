Short Bio

I am an Associate Professor in the School of Rehabilitation Sciences and Engineering at the University of Health and Rehabilitation Sciences (UHRS), and Deputy Director of the Human Resources Office. I am a Taishan Young Scholar and a recipient of the Shandong Excellent Young Scientists Fund (Overseas).

I received my Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering from the University of Science and Technology of China in 2017. I subsequently conducted postdoctoral research at UTHealth Houston / TIRR Memorial Hermann and Baylor College of Medicine. My research develops computational approaches to characterize muscle and brain activity using surface electromyography (EMG) and electroencephalography (EEG), with applications in neurorehabilitation.

Research Interests

My work combines bioelectric signal analysis, computational modeling, and experimental studies to investigate neural mechanisms and assess function in rehabilitation.

  • High-density surface EMG: motor unit activity, muscle innervation zone localization, and muscle force estimation.
  • Sleep EEG: brain state dynamics and seizure detection.
  • Neural–machine interfaces: bioelectric signal processing and rehabilitation engineering.
  • Neuromuscular control: EEG- and EMG-based assessment of motor control and functional impairment.
